# Website Privacy Notice

Effective October 1, 2026. Updated October 2, 2026. This notice describes the Digifender company website and legal hub at digifender.com, the informational OpenHost preview at openhost.pro, and related company, project and partnership correspondence.

## Scope

Digifender ("we," "us") publishes the company website, legal hub and static [OpenHost preview](https://openhost.pro/). Address privacy requests to Digifender LLC using the contact details below. Whumpo, ChapelCloud and LabThrift websites and services involve separate processing; consult the notice for the product you use. Draft product privacy notices are available for [Whumpo](/legal/whumpo/privacy), [ChapelCloud](/legal/chapelcloud/privacy) and [LabThrift](/legal/labthrift/privacy). OpenHost's [draft privacy policy](/legal/openhost/privacy) describes proposed customer-account, hosting and AI processing that is not active. OpenHost inquiries sent to Digifender are covered by this company correspondence notice. Visiting either informational site does not create a product account or accept product terms.

## Information handled when you visit

Cloudflare hosts and delivers both informational websites. Delivering and protecting a page involves technical request information, such as your IP address, requested URL, browser information, timestamps and network or security signals. Cloudflare processes traffic and security information through its network and may make operational information available to us. Its [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) explains its own processing. Neither website requires a visitor account or has a contact form, checkout or payment collection.

We have not added advertising pixels, third-party analytics scripts, embedded payment widgets, external font requests or social-media embeds to either site. Our website code does not set browser cookies or use local or session storage. An ordinary browser visit to the OpenHost preview on October 2 showed no cookies or local/session storage; that observation does not cover every visitor or security challenge. Cloudflare security features may use cookies or similar technologies depending on the security settings and challenges applied to a request. A lack of website-authored cookies does not mean no technical information is processed.

The Digifender homepage uses a local script for navigation, brand selection, motion controls and copying the company email address; it does not perform tracking or write browser storage. The OpenHost preview has no site script or form. Printer-friendly document pages include a small script that opens your browser's print dialog when you choose Print. It does not track you, store data or contact external services. PDF downloads are generated copies of the same document text and are delivered through digifender.com like other files.

## Information you send us

If you email, telephone or write to us, we receive the contact details and information you choose to provide, along with ordinary correspondence metadata. Email delivery and storage involve mail service providers. We use correspondence to respond, handle privacy or copyright requests, maintain business records and address security or legal matters. Sending an inquiry does not subscribe you to unrelated marketing.

Please send only what is needed for your request. Do not email passwords, payment-card numbers, identity documents or confidential customer records unless an appropriate handling method has been arranged. Telephone and email contact links open your chosen communications service, whose processing is subject to its own terms.

## Use and disclosure

We use information to deliver and protect the websites, diagnose faults, answer requests, administer business correspondence and meet legal obligations. Hosting and communications providers process information needed for those functions. Authorized personnel and professional advisers may access it when needed for those purposes. We may disclose relevant information when legally required or necessary to investigate abuse, protect rights or address a business transfer subject to applicable protections.

We do not sell informational-site visitor information for money or use these websites for targeted advertising. We have not added a service that tracks visitors across unrelated websites for advertising. This notice does not characterize every provider's independent processing as ours or replace the separate notices for linked products.

## Retention, security and locations

We keep correspondence and operational records for the purposes described above, including resolving requests and meeting applicable recordkeeping or legal requirements. The appropriate period depends on the record and its purpose. We have not adopted a single fixed deletion period for all company correspondence or provider records, and we do not promise immediate removal from provider backups.

Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where our providers operate. We use HTTPS to protect website traffic in transit. No website or communications method is completely secure, and this notice does not promise a particular data-residency location or certification.

## Choices and requests

You can use your browser's controls to inspect, block or clear storage. Privacy rights depend on your location and the law that applies; they may include access, correction, deletion, portability, objection, restriction or an appeal of a decision. Contact us to make a request or ask about our handling of information. We may need proportionate information to verify your request. Where an appeal is available, reply to our decision or use the subject "Privacy appeal." You may also contact the relevant privacy regulator.

These websites have no targeted-advertising or data-sale preference to toggle. We do not change their operation in response to traditional Do Not Track signals. This does not override requirements concerning legally recognized privacy signals if our processing changes.

These informational websites are intended for adults and businesses, not directed to children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.
