## Product, provider and availability

OpenHost is a hosting product being developed by Digifender LLC ("Digifender," "we," "us") for developers and small teams. A static public preview is available at [openhost.pro](https://openhost.pro/). Customer sign-in, hosting, payments and AI actions are not available there. This draft does not activate those services or create a customer agreement. The initial commercial launch is planned for the United States, billed in US dollars.

The planned catalog includes serverless applications, containers, virtual private servers, connected domains and supporting storage, databases and operational tools. Services would use upstream infrastructure, with Cloudflare and DigitalOcean as the preferred providers. Features, locations and reseller arrangements remain subject to verification. A product description or planning price is not an available offer.

The Digifender company website has its own [website terms](/legal/terms) and [privacy notice](/legal/privacy). These OpenHost drafts do not govern Whumpo, ChapelCloud or LabThrift. Any preview access must identify its actual scope and applicable agreement separately.

The cancellation process must identify those resources, their remaining costs and the action needed to remove them.
