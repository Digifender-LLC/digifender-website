# Copyright and Legal Contact

Contact information updated October 2, 2026.

## Report a concern

For a copyright concern about Digifender, Whumpo, ChapelCloud, LabThrift, OpenHost or a website hosted through a Digifender product, email [legal@digifender.com](mailto:legal@digifender.com). Include the exact URL, a description of the material, the right or issue involved, and a way to contact you. Other abuse or privacy concerns can use the same address; you do not have to claim copyright ownership to report another issue.

Send only the information needed to locate and evaluate the concern. Do not attach suspected child sexual abuse material, nonconsensual intimate images, passwords or unnecessary personal information. A report may need to be shared with the affected party or advisers to investigate it. See the [Website Privacy Notice](/legal/privacy) and, for product processing, the relevant [product privacy document](/legal).

## Copyright-agent status

**No DMCA designated-agent registration has been filed for Digifender LLC.** The company contacts on this page are available for reports; they are not presented as an approved outside copyright agent or a completed Copyright Office designation. The LLC's registered-agent role does not, by itself, appoint that service as a copyright agent.

The [draft Whumpo Copyright and Abuse Policy](/legal/whumpo/copyright) describes the proposed notice and counter-notice process and identifies the appointment work still pending. The [draft OpenHost Copyright and Abuse Policy](/legal/openhost/copyright) records its proposed process and pending appointment status as well. Neither draft is a representation that all conditions for DMCA safe-harbor protection have been met. The [US Copyright Office](https://www.copyright.gov/512/) explains the designation and other Section 512 conditions.
