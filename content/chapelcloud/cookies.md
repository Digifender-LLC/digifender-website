# ChapelCloud Cookie Notice

Draft updated October 1, 2026. Effective date: pending adoption. This notice concerns the public pages at chapel.cloud; separately contracted software or customer systems need their own applicable inventory.

## Current public website

The public website provides service information and does not require sign-in. A fresh-browser review of the homepage and representative software, hardware and managed-service pages found no cookies or application entries in local storage or session storage. Scripts, fonts and images were requested from the ChapelCloud website; no third-party advertising, analytics or payment embed was identified in that session.

These observations describe the pages and session reviewed. They are not a promise that every security challenge, browser or future feature uses identical storage. Your browser may cache page resources without creating an application cookie.

## Cloudflare security

Cloudflare delivers and protects the site. Its security features may set cookies when a particular protection or challenge is applied. For example, a clearance cookie can record a completed security check. We do not claim that every cookie listed in Cloudflare's documentation is used here, or assign a fixed lifetime to settings that have not been confirmed.

See [Cloudflare's cookie documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/fundamentals/reference/policies-compliances/cloudflare-cookies/) for provider purposes and behavior. Technical request and security information can be processed even when the browser receives no cookie.

## Your choices and changes

You can inspect, block or remove stored website data in your browser. Blocking security storage may interfere with a challenge or page access. Clearing browser data does not erase correspondence or provider-side records.

Before adding technologies that require consent or opt-out controls, we must update the inventory and provide the controls required for the actual processing. This notice does not provide blanket consent to future tracking.
