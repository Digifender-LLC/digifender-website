DIGIFENDER — BRAND MARK FAMILY

Start with digifender-horizontal-signature.svg for light backgrounds and
digifender-horizontal-glacier.svg for dark backgrounds. Both files are in
the horizontal/ folder. The matching standalone symbols are in symbols/.

The approved geometry is preserved exactly. Color, scale, framing, and
wordmark placement are the only changes. All 17 ridges remain separate.

USAGE
Use the Signature gradient for the primary identity on light backgrounds.
Use Glacier on dark backgrounds. Use Cobalt when one digital color is
preferred, Ink for documents, and Black or White for single-color work.
Keep the wordmark solid; gradient color belongs to the symbol.

Allow clear space of at least 20% of the symbol height around the entire
lockup, beyond the SVG canvas where needed. Recommended full-symbol size
is at least 48 CSS px high or 12 mm in print. Proof at the final size.
This family does not include a separately simplified small-size mark.

SVG lettering is outlined, so no font installation is required to use
the assets. Font files are included for future design work.
SVG paths remain editable. Resize the entire artwork proportionally.
PNG exports are sRGB. Print workflows should be proofed with the printer.
