Both gradients run continuously from upper left to lower right across the entire symbol. Use a solid wordmark beside them.

Use with consistency

Clear space

Allow at least 20% of the symbol height around a complete lockup, beyond the SVG canvas where needed.

Minimum size

Recommended full symbol height: 48 CSS px for digital use, 12 mm in print. Proof at final size; no simplified small-size symbol is included.

Backgrounds

Use Signature, Cobalt, Ink, or Black on light backgrounds. Use Glacier or White on Midnight or comparably dark, quiet backgrounds.

Gradient

Gradient runs upper left to lower right, continuously across the whole mark.

Geometry

Scale proportionally. Preserve path data, stroke weight, and ridge gaps.

Exports

Transparent SVG and PNG for symbols and lockups. App icons deliberately include a background. PNGs use sRGB colors; proof with the printer before CMYK production.

Typography: Manrope at weight 650, with restrained spacing. The supplied vector wordmarks are the master artwork. Font files and the SIL Open Font License are included for future design work.
