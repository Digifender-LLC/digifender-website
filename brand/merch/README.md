Clear space of 20% of symbol height is respected on every artboard (checked by script). The decal, cut at the mark edge, is sized flush; keep 20% of symbol height (0.56 in) free when applying it. Lanyard is wordmark-only, so the 12 mm symbol minimum does not apply; the wordmark is 9.1 mm high and spaced at least 20% of its height apart.

New text (website "digifender.com" on tote, mug, decal) is Manrope 650 converted to outlines, set as plain type and not as the wordmark.

## Embroidery notes (polo and cap patch)
- No gradients; every colour is flat. Polo one-colour version uses 1 thread colour, flat version 2, patch 2 thread colours plus the twill (all fewer than 6).
- No strokes under 0.5 pt. Thinnest features are the 48-unit ridge stroke (about 0.16 in at 3.5 in wide) and the wordmark stems (about 1.7 mm); the ridge gaps are the tightest detail, so the embroiderer must confirm they stitch cleanly at this size or enlarge.
- The symbol is built from strokes; the digitiser converts them to stitches.
- Thread colours: White (#FFFFFF) and Cyan (#12B7D5) to be matched by the embroiderer to the nearest thread chart colour; patch twill Midnight (#09172B). No thread brand or code has been chosen.

## Print notes
- Tote, mug: Signature gradient needs a full-colour process (DTG, sublimation); screen print needs separations. Paper (#F4F7FB) is a preview fill; set the fabric or mug colour with the supplier. Confirm mug handle position against the supplier template (art is centred).
- Sticker: the CutContour circle is 3 in; the Midnight bleed circle extends 0.0625 in beyond it. Name or convert the magenta stroke to the spot colour the cutter requires.
- Lanyard: 18 in length is a working choice; confirm repeat length and sublimation area with the supplier.
- Decal: strokes in the symbol must be expanded to outlines (Illustrator Outline Stroke, Inkscape Stroke to Path) before cutting; this was not done here because no tool was available to do so without altering the artwork. The decal has several loose pieces, so weeding and transfer tape are needed.
